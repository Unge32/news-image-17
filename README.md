A one-page newspaper front page summing up the world's news of Tuesday 17 November 2026 in a single 1024x1024 image.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.

## The image

Six stories, all of which happened or were first reported on Tuesday 17 November 2026:

1. **Ukraine hits the Crimea bridge again** — a second strike in two days badly damaged the road link to occupied Crimea.
2. **Iran fires drones at a U.S. radar in Bahrain** — the attack on the Sheikh Isa base was intercepted, with no casualties or damage.
3. **Japan scrambles jets as Russian planes enter its airspace** — Tokyo lodged a strong protest.
4. **China and Canada agree tariff cuts** — Xi Jinping and Prime Minister Mark Carney pledge deeper trade ties.
5. **U.K. sanctions eight Israeli builders over West Bank settlements** — plus a group accused of blocking aid to Gaza.
6. **Rocket fault blamed for loss of 40 Amazon satellites** — ULA says early data points to its Vulcan rocket's first stage.

Sources: Reuters, Al Jazeera, CNN, GeekWire, Just Security.
